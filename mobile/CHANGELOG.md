## 0.0.2

* Initial release of the DMF Logo SDK for Flutter.
* Physics-based kinetic animated splash screen (`DmfSplashScreen`).
* Dual brand mark styles: `LogoStyle.dmfOneLine` and `LogoStyle.dmfFullLogo`.
* Dual luxury themes (warm cream light and deep obsidian dark).
* Bundled official brand assets and variable typography (Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans).
